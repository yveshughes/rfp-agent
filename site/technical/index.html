01 / THE WORKFLOW

Find. Prepare. Review.

  1. 01

    Import your company’s experience

    Extract reusable evidence from previous responses. Keep each claim connected to its source document and page.

  2. 02

    Find relevant opportunities

    Browse sources by location and agency. Read the actual RFP, extract requirements, and compare them with the company’s evidence.

  3. 03

    Prepare an honest draft

    Use supported experience, cite it, and flag qualifications that still need confirmation. Missing evidence stays visible.

  4. 04

    Revise by voice. Approve the changes.

    Turn a spoken request into a proposed edit. Inspect the diff, apply or undo, and export a draft for human review and submission.

02 / THE ARCHITECTURE

Built around Vultr.

Planned for the Future of Work track, with NetBird for controlled access.

VULTR

The system of record and control

A VM hosts the API, background jobs, saved evidence, proposal versions, and generated files. Work persists across sessions.

AI

Reasoning with bounded tools

The backend retrieves documents, compares requirements, and drafts responses. Vultr Serverless Inference is the preferred model provider, subject to validation.

NETBIRD

Protected access to company knowledge

The planned reverse proxy provides authenticated access without public inbound application ports on the app VM.

YOU

Approval before submission

The agent prepares the response. You confirm qualifications, approve edits, and decide what is submitted.

03 / WHAT MAKES THIS WORTH BUILDING

Useful autonomy. Inspectable work.

EXECUTION

A workflow that produces an artifact

The intended agent fetches an RFP, extracts requirements, compares evidence, and produces an editable response. A saved run connects the input documents to the output version.

Proof to show: a real run, its source pages, and its generated draft.

TRUST

Knowing when the evidence is missing

A prior proposal can establish relevant experience without proving every new qualification. Missing, dated, or unverified claims stay flagged for confirmation.

Proof to show: one supported claim beside one unresolved requirement.

HUMAN INTERACTION

Voice editing you can inspect and undo

A spoken request proposes a change. The owner sees the diff and decides whether to apply it. Version history preserves earlier drafts.

Proof to show: speak → inspect → apply → undo, on a real response.

VULTR

The infrastructure does the work

The planned Vultr backend owns orchestration, job state, company evidence, and output files. Modest CPU compute handles document jobs; model calls stay in the trusted backend.

Proof to show: the running VM, persisted results after refresh, and measured processing time.

NETBIRD

Private company knowledge deserves controlled access

Authenticated reverse-proxy access is the planned NetBird integration. It has a practical purpose: giving a reviewer access while keeping inbound application ports closed on the app VM.

Proof to show: an authorized review session and the actual network configuration.

BUSINESS IMPACT

More capacity to pursue the right work

The long-term vision is continuous opportunity monitoring and reusable company knowledge. The initial demo focuses on one complete response workflow; nationwide ranking and automatic submission are outside its scope.

Measure: evidence coverage, edits required, run time, and actual operating cost.
